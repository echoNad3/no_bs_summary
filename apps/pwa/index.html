How this works

  1. Enter the shared app password once.
  2. Paste a YouTube link, or share a video to this app from Android.
  3. Tap Cut the BS. Completed summaries are cached in the cloud, so repeating the same video and caption language does not spend another TranscriptAPI or Gemini request.

Missing from the Android share menu?

Install this app from Chrome using Install app. A normal home-screen shortcut is not enough. After an app update, uninstall and reinstall it if Android still has the old share settings.

In ReVanced, open the full Android system share sheet using More. If your build has a custom “Change share sheet” option, disable it.
